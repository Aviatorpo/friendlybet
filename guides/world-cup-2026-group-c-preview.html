World Cup 2026 Group C Preview: Brazil, Morocco, Scotland & Haiti

Everything you need to call Group C correctly in your prediction pool — the contenders, the dangers, and the most likely order of finish.

Group C of the 2026 World Cup pairs one of the tournament's biggest names with a rising force, a proud European side and a debutant-spirited outsider. For anyone filling in a prediction bracket, it is a group with a clear favourite at the top but a genuinely open race for the qualifying places below. Here is how the four teams stack up — and how to turn that read into points.

Who tops the group?

Brazil are the obvious pick for first place. Their squad combines elite attacking talent with strength in midfield and at the back, and across a three-game group stage that quality usually tells. For a prediction pool, putting Brazil top of Group C is the low-risk, high-confidence call — but it is also the call everyone else will make, so it rarely wins you the group on its own.

The fight for second

This is where Group C is actually decided. Morocco are the natural pick for the runners-up spot: well-organised, hard to break down and carrying real belief after their recent deep run on the world stage. Scotland, however, are exactly the kind of disciplined, physical side that can frustrate a favourite and grind out the results needed to sneak through. If you want a differentiator in your pool, backing Scotland to pip Morocco for second is the bold move that could separate you from the pack.

Can the underdog cause a shock?

Haiti arrive as the clear outsiders, but expanded 48-team World Cups reward teams that can steal a single result — and with eight of the best third-placed teams advancing, even a group's fourth seed is rarely completely out of it after one good night. Predicting a Haiti upset is high-risk, but in a scoring system that rewards bold correct calls, it is the kind of pick that can win a whole pool.
