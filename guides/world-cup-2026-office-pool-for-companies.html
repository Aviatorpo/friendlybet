How to run a free World Cup 2026 office pool for your company

A World Cup is the easiest excuse all year to get a whole company talking. Here is how to run a prediction pool for your team that takes 30 seconds to set up and asks nobody for an email, a password or a payment.

Create a Free World Cup Pool in 30 Seconds

FriendlyBet Live is a free, open-source, privacy-first World Cup 2026 prediction pool for friends, offices and tech teams — no signup, no ads, no money, no personal data. That combination is exactly what makes it safe to drop into a work Slack: there is no account for colleagues to create, no email list to manage, and no money changing hands, so it sidesteps the usual HR, IT and compliance worries that kill most office sweepstakes before they start.

Why most office pools are a hassle — and this one isn't

The traditional office pool is a spreadsheet that one person babysits, chases people to fill in, and tallies by hand every night. The app alternatives usually want everyone to register with a work email and then market to them forever. Neither is a good fit for a modern company, least of all a privacy-conscious tech team.

FriendlyBet removes all of that. Colleagues join with a nickname, predictions lock automatically when the first match kicks off, and a deterministic scoring job recalculates the live leaderboard for you. No spreadsheet, no arguments, no data collection.

Good for IT & security: there is no signup, no email harvesting and no personally identifiable information stored. Account recovery uses a 16-character code that is SHA-256 hashed in the browser, so there is no sensitive data to leak. The whole app is open source (MIT) and can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure if you prefer.

Set it up in four steps

  1. Create a pool. Open FriendlyBet, pick a nickname, and create a pool named after your team or company.
  2. Share one link. Drop the join link or 5-character code into Slack, Teams, or the office WhatsApp group.
  3. Everyone predicts. Each person ranks the 12 groups, builds the full knockout bracket, and calls the Golden Boot top scorer.
  4. Climb the leaderboard. Scores update automatically as real results arrive. The sharpest forecaster wins the bragging rights.

Make it a real company-wide event

Pin the join link in your main channel, set a friendly deadline before the opening match, and post the leaderboard screenshot after each round. Because the bracket each person builds is generated from their own group predictions, every colleague has a genuinely different bracket — which keeps the banter going right up to the Final.

Office pool FAQ

Is there a free World Cup pool for tech companies?
Yes — FriendlyBet is free, open-source, and needs no signup, which is exactly what a privacy-conscious tech team wants.
Do colleagues need to create accounts?
No. They join with a nickname. No email, phone or OAuth, and no personal data is stored.
Is it gambling?
No money is handled in the app. It is a prediction game for fun and bragging rights, which keeps it workplace-appropriate.
Can we self-host it on our own servers?
Yes. It is MIT-licensed static files — clone it, point it at your own Supabase project, and deploy anywhere.
