# Playbook: CEO Board Reporting

Use this for CEO summaries to Eyal.

## Default Format

Keep reports concise. Include only sections that matter.

- Decision: the current recommendation or outcome.
- Progress: what was done.
- Risks: meaningful product, technical, privacy, cost, legal, or quality risks.
- Validation: checks run or still missing.
- Needs Eyal: chairman-level decision, if any.
- Next move: what the CEO will do next.

## Rules

- Do not bury blockers.
- Separate facts from judgment.
- Mention failed or skipped validation.
- Say when no Eyal decision is needed.
- Avoid department-by-department minutes unless Eyal asks.
