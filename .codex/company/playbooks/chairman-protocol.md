# Playbook: Chairman Protocol

Use this whenever the CEO or Executive Office interacts with Eyal on broad work.

## Eyal Owns

- Vision and values
- Brand identity and product taste
- Monetization philosophy
- Meaningful cost/risk approval
- Strategic pivots
- Legal or reputation risk tolerance

## CEO Owns

- Translating goals into work
- Routing departments
- Resolving cross-functional disagreement
- Executing or coordinating execution
- Validating outcomes
- Reporting progress and decisions needed

## Ask Eyal Only For

- Strategic direction
- Brand/taste decision
- Irreversible or high-risk change
- Meaningful recurring cost
- Legal or public-trust risk
- Explicit preference where no responsible default exists

## Do Not Ask Eyal For

- File locations discoverable in the repo
- Routine implementation choices
- Test command selection
- Minor copy variants unless tone is brand-defining
- Department routing

## Output

Return:

- Chairman-level decision needed, if any
- Recommended default if Eyal does not decide
- Work the company can continue without him
