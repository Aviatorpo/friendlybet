# Decision: Agent Domain Mastery Training

Date: 2026-06-23

## Context

Eyal asked the CEO to raise all FriendlyBet agents to a much higher level in their own worlds of content, including Engineering, QA, HR, and the wider department list.

## Decision

FriendlyBet will treat agent training as an operating cadence, not a one-time motivational instruction. The CEO owns the work, HR And Agent Excellence owns standards and feedback loops, Executive owns routing, and FinOps Enablement owns concise documentation hygiene.

## Operating Rule

When agent quality or domain expertise is the request, use `agent-domain-mastery-training.md` to create department-specific study, practice, validation, and memory-update artifacts.

## Guardrails

- Keep training artifacts short, practical, and tied to real FriendlyBet cases.
- Prefer repo docs, skills, tests, and playbooks before adding tools or subagents.
- Verify current, official, legal, pricing, provider, sports, SEO, or high-stakes claims before relying on them.
- Ask Eyal only for chairman-level choices: values, brand, legal/reputation risk, meaningful recurring cost, irreversible strategy, or personal taste.

## Next Step

Run the batch plan in `../agent-mastery-roadmap.md`, starting with Engineering, QA And Release, Sports Data And Rules, Privacy Security, and HR And Agent Excellence because they protect the highest-risk product surfaces.
