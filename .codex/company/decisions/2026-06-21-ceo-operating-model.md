# Decision: CEO Operating Model

Date: 2026-06-21

## Decision

Add `friendlybet-company-ceo` as the primary front door for broad or strategic work. Eyal operates as Chairman. The CEO owns routing, execution, validation, and board-style reporting.

## Rationale

Eyal wants to avoid CEO-level management and implementation detail. A single CEO interface lets him express goals while the virtual company handles department coordination and asks only for board-level decisions.

## Consequences

- Broad work should start with the CEO skill.
- Add chairman protocol, decision rights, operating cadences, CEO reporting, quality gates, and agent performance review playbooks.
- HR Agent Excellence becomes the guardian of agent quality under the CEO operating model.
