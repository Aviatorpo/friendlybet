# Handoff: Content To Product, Design, Engineering, QA, And Privacy

Use this when Content discovers a story pattern, live-desk need, share-card opportunity, or public wording risk.

## Content Must Provide

- Story type: vindication, collapse, chaos, trap, drama, history, or next-match stakes.
- Required data fields.
- Required freshness.
- Hebrew and English copy shape.
- Pool/prediction angle.
- Public/privacy risk.
- Fallback when data is missing.

## Product Needs

- What user moment is being improved.
- Whether the story belongs in Pundit, dashboard, share card, story carousel, leaderboard, or social video.
- What is MVP and what is out of scope.

## Design Needs

- Text length in Hebrew and English.
- Directionality.
- Visual hierarchy.
- Face/text safe zones for story images or videos.
- Mobile fit and compact control risk.

## Engineering Needs

- Exact data contract.
- Whether data is already available.
- Freshness and cache requirements.
- Whether a provider or generated snapshot is needed.
- Failure/fallback behavior.

## QA Needs

- Changed copy and surfaces.
- Stale-data risk.
- Missing-data fallback.
- Hebrew/English parity.
- Public share behavior.

## Privacy Needs

- Whether user names or pool picks appear.
- Whether the surface is public or private.
- Whether wording implies real-money betting.

## Bad Handoff

- "Make this more dramatic" with no data requirement.
- Copy that needs unavailable data.
- Hebrew too long for the designed surface.
- Public user-specific copy without privacy review.
