# Handoff: Product, Design, And Growth To Engineering And Content

Use this when a product, UX, or growth idea becomes a buildable feature, screen, or public-facing change.

## Product Must Provide

- User job.
- MVP.
- Out-of-scope list.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Scoring, lock, rule, and data dependencies.
- Privacy and cost assumptions.

## Design Must Provide

- Target screen or flow.
- Mobile/RTL requirements.
- Text-fit constraints.
- Accessibility notes.
- Component/state expectations.
- Visual hierarchy and interaction risks.

## Growth Must Provide

- Public audience.
- Trust promise.
- SEO/open-source purpose.
- No-tracker/no-dark-pattern assurance.
- README/contributor impact when relevant.

## Engineering Needs

- What to build first.
- What not to build.
- Data contract and state model.
- Version/cache expectations.
- Rollback/fallback.

## Content Needs

- Which copy surfaces change.
- Hebrew and English tone.
- What public claims are safe.
- What story or share angle the product enables.

## QA Needs

- Acceptance criteria.
- Critical paths.
- Edge cases.
- Release risk.

## Bad Handoff

- Pretty mockup with no state model.
- Growth objective with no user value.
- Feature request without lock/scoring implications.
- Acceptance criteria that QA cannot test.
