# Handoff: Privacy And FinOps To All Departments

Use this when work touches data exposure, public copy, money-like language, providers, paid tooling, automation, or operational load.

## Privacy Must Teach

- What data is collected.
- Where it is stored.
- Who can read it.
- What public surfaces expose it.
- Whether RLS or snapshots are involved.
- Which wording creates gambling/legal ambiguity.
- Safer product or copy alternative.

## FinOps Must Teach

- Recurring cost.
- Rate limits.
- Operational burden.
- Failure mode.
- Cheaper alternative.
- Whether Eyal approval is required.

## All Departments Must Know

- FriendlyBet must not handle money, deposits, payouts, escrow, odds trading, or bookmaker-like behavior.
- Optional voluntary tips are the only monetization direction unless Eyal changes it.
- Live data and generated media can become expensive quickly.
- Public trust is a product feature.

## Escalate To Eyal

- Legal or reputation risk.
- Meaningful recurring cost.
- Monetization change.
- Public brand/taste decision.
- Irreversible operational commitment.
