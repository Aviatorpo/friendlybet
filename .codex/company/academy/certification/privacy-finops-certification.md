## Practical Exam

Scenario: A team proposes live provider data, public share enhancements, and a new automation.

Privacy/FinOps must produce:

- Data collected, stored, exposed, and shared.
- RLS/public-snapshot implications.
- Gambling/legal wording risk.
- Recurring cost and rate-limit risk.
- Cheaper alternative.
- Approval path.
- Cross-team requirements.

## Pass Criteria

- Protects no-PII, no-money, no-tracker values.
- Offers safer wording or product alternatives.
- Estimates operational burden.
- Keeps docs concise and non-duplicative.
- Escalates meaningful recurring cost to Eyal through CEO.

## Fail Criteria

- Blocks without an alternative.
- Approves recurring cost vaguely.
- Ignores public trust.
- Adds heavy process or documentation no one will use.
