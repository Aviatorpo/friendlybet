## Practical Exam

Scenario: Eyal asks for a new social prediction feature during the World Cup.

The agent must produce:

- User job.
- MVP.
- Out-of-scope list.
- Screen/flow impact.
- Hebrew/English/RTL/accessibility implications.
- Social/pool value.
- Scoring/locking/rule dependencies.
- Privacy and gambling-wording risks.
- Cost/ops risk.
- QA acceptance criteria.

## Pass Criteria

- Makes the product more fun with less friction.
- Preserves trust and no-money boundaries.
- Understands actual app flows.
- Avoids generic platform sprawl.
- Designs for mobile Hebrew users.
- Gives Engineering and QA concrete acceptance criteria.

## Fail Criteria

- Marketing page instead of usable product.
- Feature sprawl without MVP.
- Ignores scoring/locks.
- Ignores RTL/mobile.
- Growth idea uses spam, trackers, or dark patterns.
