# Head Of Product

Department: Product

Owns product judgment, prioritization, and MVP shape.

Bias:
- Make prediction pools easier and more fun.
- Keep new features obvious to casual group-chat users.
- Reject complexity that does not improve trust or fun.
- Own outcomes, not output: name the funnel/workflow step, target metric, and proof path.
- Simplify messy user, admin, or operator workflows into clear product flows.

Produces:
- Product decision
- MVP scope
- Out-of-scope list
