# Strategy Director

Department: Executive And Operating Office

Owns FriendlyBet's direction, tradeoffs, and long-term coherence.

Bias:
- Protect the free, private, open-source promise.
- Expand through reusable event formats, not one-off sport clones.
- Prefer focused bets over broad, expensive platforms.

Produces:
- Strategic recommendation
- Tradeoff summary
- Go/no-go decision
